How Did We Get Here?

Every great idea starts somewhere.

We started off as a bunch of bumbling idiots, and well, that hasn't changed one bit. It's just that now, we're just some bumbling idiots with that have streaming set ups!


Several ideas were thrown at the wall when we were deciding what we wanted to stream, but eventually we all agreed on having us stream ourselves play. But then the next question was, what game to play? More idea were thrown at the wall and this time it landed on as of now, a very controversial


That game being Roblox.

🎥 The Interface We Envisioned

The Interface We Envisioned

A glimpse of our ideal streaming setup.

We wanted our streaming interface to have a the theme of a bygone era. A time much more simplier, a time where a certain tune would play whenever you start up your computer, a time before any of us were born!


The asthetic that we went with is Frutiger Aero. From what Wikipedia, The Free Encyclopedia says: "is a design style that was prevalent from the mid-2000s to the early 2010s."


We wanted to use Frutiger Aero because of it's unique style. The bright images and colorful designs often bring out a sense of wonder and whimsy. Of a time where computers where someting new and exciting.
